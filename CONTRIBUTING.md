# Contributing

Submit only material you own or are authorized to contribute. Identify its provenance, any third-party material and required notices, and any AI assistance. Do not include client material, private operational data or third-party code without the rights and notices needed for its inclusion.

Eligible submissions are contributed under the same [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE) and applicable [notice and scope](NOTICE). This repository is source-available, not OSI-approved open source. The official license defines permitted purposes; these contribution instructions do not narrow them. Disclose any material you cannot offer under those terms before submitting it.

Contributors retain copyright in their own protectable contributions. A contribution does not automatically assign copyright or grant Michael E. Ayotte rights to offer contributor-owned material under a separate commercial license. Such material requires separate written rights from its copyright holder before commercial licensing. No copyright is claimed in uncopyrightable AI-generated portions.

For proposed commercial use outside the license's permitted purposes, contact [Michael E. Ayotte](https://github.com/MichaelEAyotte). Any separate permission must be in writing and must cover all necessary rights; the repository owner cannot grant rights owned by other contributors without their authorization.

The [earlier MIT release and permissions](PUBLICATION.md) remain preserved, including commercial use and redistribution under MIT's terms. These contribution terms do not revoke those permissions.

For changes to the evaluator, run `npm test` and `npm run demo`. Keep fictional examples and the AI-assisted synthetic-code disclosure accurate. These checks do not establish live ATLAS or model acceptance.
